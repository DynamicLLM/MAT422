# Representative Codex Prompt: Problem Formulation

> I am building a MAT 422 project to predict whether a student will meet a target final-course performance threshold. My available variables are study hours, attendance rate, prior GPA, and assignment average. The target is binary. I must avoid data leakage, use five-fold cross-validation, compare at least two models, and report accuracy, F1, precision, recall, and ROC-AUC. Please restate the problem, identify the prediction target, candidate inputs, constraints, and measurable success criteria. Ask me about any critical missing assumptions instead of inventing them.
