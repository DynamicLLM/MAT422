# AI Critic Skill
Ask: Did the solution address the original problem? Were assumptions justified? Was the model appropriate? Were constraints satisfied? Could leakage, overfitting, numerical error, or bias matter? Did software execute as expected? Are reported values traceable to actual outputs? Is the result reproducible? What would invalidate the conclusion?

Deliverable: short critique documenting at least three risks, limitations, or alternative interpretations and how they were addressed.
