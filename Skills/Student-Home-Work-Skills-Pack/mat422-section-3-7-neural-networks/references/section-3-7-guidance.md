## Assignment philosophy

The homework should demonstrate understanding through selected examples rather than a fixed problem set. Students may use these examples as a starting point, but they should personalize values, comments, visuals, and reflection before submission.

## Suggested notebook structure

1. Title and student/course information.
2. Imports and setup.
3. One section for each assigned topic.
4. Numeric or visual verification for the main claims.
5. Reflection and submission checklist.

## Quality bar

A good notebook should be runnable top to bottom, visually readable on GitHub, and understandable to a peer who is new to the topic. Use markdown to state the math idea, code to test it, and a sentence after the output to interpret the result.
